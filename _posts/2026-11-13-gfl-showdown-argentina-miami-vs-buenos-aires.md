The Global Fight League makes its debut Friday, December 18, at Microestadio Antonio Rotili in Buenos Aires, Argentina. The opening event pits the Buenos Aires Gauchos against the Miami Monsters in ten fights, with former Bellator fighter Grant Neal and UFC veteran Omar Morales among the more familiar names on the card.

GFL was originally supposed to launch in 2025 with a roster that included several former UFC champions, but its planned events were canceled. The December debut looks considerably different, with much of the card made up of fighters from the regional circuit.

## How the GFL works

The GFL uses a team format. Each fight earns points for the winner's team, and the overall result is determined by the combined scores. Under the league's published system, a finish earns **four points**, a decision win **three**, a draw **two**, a decision loss **one**, and a stoppage loss **zero**. That means finishing an opponent is worth more than winning a decision, while a fighter who goes the distance can still contribute a point in defeat.

The opening card runs from middleweight at 185 pounds down to women's atomweight at 105 pounds, including several catchweight bouts.
