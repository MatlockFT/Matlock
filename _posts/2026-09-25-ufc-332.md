**Natalia Silva** is a fast, movement-heavy striker with a taekwondo base who does a great job changing angles, mixing kicks and staying hard to read. She’s very comfortable fighting at range, but she’s also a legit submission threat if the fight hits the mat. She’s won 14 straight and is coming off wins over Rose Namajunas, Alexa Grasso and Jessica Andrade.

**Wang Cong** is a polished kickboxer and sanda fighter with very sharp hands, good timing and a pretty composed counter-striking game. She’s accurate, physically strong and usually does her best work when she can keep things technical on the feet. She’s won four straight since her only career loss, most recently beating Tracy Cortez.

**Deiveson Figueiredo** is a compact orthodox fighter with big power, sharp counters and a dangerous submission game. He’s still very explosive in short bursts and does a good job mixing hard low kicks with sudden level changes, but at bantamweight he’s been a little more measured than he was at 125. He’s coming off back-to-back losses to Umar Nurmagomedov and Song Yadong.

**Payton Talbott** is a tall, fluid switch-stance striker who likes to build combinations as the fight goes on. He throws a lot of volume, works well off straight punches and is dangerous when he starts getting opponents backed up, while still being comfortable scrambling or attacking on the ground. He’s rebounded from the Barcelos loss with decision wins over Felipe Lima and Henry Cejudo.
