**Aieza Ramos Bertolso** is a long, well-rounded strawweight, but grappling is clearly her best area. She’s good at working into top position, scrambles well and is constantly hunting submissions, while also having a pretty useful kicking and clinch game on the feet. She’s 7-0 as a pro, the LFA champion, and has four submission wins.

**Camila Reynoso** is an aggressive, tough strawweight who keeps a good pace and is comfortable mixing striking with grappling. She likes to scramble and hunt finishes on the ground, but she can also put combinations together and has four TKOs on her record. She’s won three straight, most recently finishing Maria Luiza late in the third round.

**George Staines** is a very complete lightweight with a strong wrestling and grappling base. He pressures well, chains takedowns together, scrambles extremely well and is constantly looking to take the back, while his striking is more about clean volume and staying defensively responsible than huge power. He’s 10-0 as a pro after an excellent 16-1 amateur run and is coming off a second-round submission of Conor McCarthy.
