UFC Vegas 124 takes place Saturday, November 7, at Meta APEX in Las Vegas. The prelims begin at 5 p.m. ET (4 p.m. CT), followed by the main card at 8 p.m. ET (7 p.m. CT). The full card streams on Paramount+.

Waldo Cortes-Acosta meets Johnny Walker in a short-notice heavyweight main event after Sean Brady withdrew from his scheduled fight with Gabriel Bonfim. The Austin Bashi–Lucas Brennan bout also changed, with Carlos Calderon taking Brennan's place on short notice. Walker is coming off his first heavyweight appearance, while Cortes-Acosta gets an opportunity to headline. Tatiana Suarez and Virna Jandiroba meet in the women's strawweight co-main event.

**Waldo Cortes-Acosta** is a heavyweight boxer who relies on combinations, timing and a steady pace rather than hunting a single knockout punch. His offense is built around working from striking range and keeping opponents occupied with volume.

**Johnny Walker** is an unpredictable, long-range striker whose offense features kicks, knees and explosive bursts from awkward angles. His first heavyweight appearance has given him a different physical matchup from the light heavyweight competition he faced for most of his UFC career.
