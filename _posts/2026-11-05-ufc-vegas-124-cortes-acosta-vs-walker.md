UFC Vegas 124 takes place Saturday, November 7, at Meta APEX in Las Vegas. The prelims begin at 5 p.m. ET (4 p.m. CT), followed by the main card at 8 p.m. ET (7 p.m. CT). The full card streams on Paramount+.

Waldo Cortes-Acosta meets Johnny Walker in a short-notice heavyweight main event after Sean Brady withdrew from his scheduled fight with Gabriel Bonfim. Walker is coming off his first heavyweight appearance, while Cortes-Acosta gets an opportunity to headline. Tatiana Suarez and Virna Jandiroba meet in the women's strawweight co-main event.

**Waldo Cortes-Acosta** is a heavyweight boxer who prefers to work from a striking base, Cortes-Acosta relies on combinations, timing and his ability to maintain a steady pace rather than hunting a single knockout punch.

**Johnny Walker** is an unpredictable, long-range striker whose offense features kicks, knees and explosive bursts from awkward angles. His first heavyweight appearance has given him a different physical matchup from the light heavyweight competition he faced for most of his UFC career.
