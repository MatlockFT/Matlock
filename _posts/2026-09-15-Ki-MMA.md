What does the MMA ecosystem at large lack in 2026 and beyond? Is it a very long, tournament mixed in with irrelevant "super fights"? How about story telling? Has MMA become stale because of the UFC's relentless schedule mixed with their own feeder programming such as DWCS, TUF, and Road To UFC? Do MMA fans long for the days of yore when all the best fighters were in Japan fighting in a ring? Perhaps days of yore for some is a one-night tournament.
