What is missing in the MMA ecosystem? This is a very difficult question to answer because it leads to another question. What do MMA fans want?

There are many, many answers to this question and many of those are in opposition to each other. Ring vs. Cage. One organization vs. many organizations. 

Scott Coker feels like the modern day MMA landscape primarily lacks stars and storytelling. He also feels like fans are nostalgic for the days of tournaments and feeling that PRIDE gave us with the ring, blue gloves, and white canvas. With todays MMA market being completely dominated by the UFC and their relentless schedule, its tough for them to focus on building stars and even tough for fans of MMA to stay up to date with what's going on. Coker feels like he can solve that problem by providing even MORE MMA by building an organization that has a 32-man tournament spread across 13 events in 4 different regions. Thus, Scott Coker holds a press event to announce this new organization to tell everyone... well... that this will be a 32-fighter tournament spread across 13 events in 4 different regions and the first one with by a featherweight tournament starting in Q1 of 2027.

No information about who they've signed because they haven't signed anyone.
No information about where people can watch it because they haven't secured anything.
No secured event date, venue, or location. They said probably San Jose, CA.

They did talk about about several other details but the big, meaty stuff to chew on is yet to come.

Personally, I have my skepticisms about this. I'm not a tournament format hater but I'm also not too fond of it. I like the idea of multiple fights in one night but that comes with immense risk as fighters can fall out. Scotty Cokes doesn't seem to care though because Daniel Cormier was an alternate! And look what he did!

Scott Coker wants the tournament format because he thinks it builds or finds stars. 

I hate that word. Stars.
An ill-defined word. Who does Scott Coker consider to be a star and more importantly, in 2026 can he name a star in MMA? Are we talking someone who can sell PPV? Is Ki MMA going to sell PPV?

I like the ring, I like the gloves, I like the concept, I like Scott Coker, and I even don't mind Ki MMA as a name. I am rooting for this brand to succeed in whatever way they can.
