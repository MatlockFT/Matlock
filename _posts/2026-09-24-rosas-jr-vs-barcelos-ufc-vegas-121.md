**Vanessa Demopoulos** is a pressure-oriented strawweight with a jiu-jitsu-first game, especially dangerous off her back with armbars, triangles and an active guard. She’s scrappy rather than especially polished on the feet, throws with volume, and is generally willing to turn fights into messy exchanges where she can create grappling opportunities.

She enters her fight with Yazmin Jauregui on September 26 trying to snap a three-fight losing streak: a first-round armbar loss to Jaqueline Amorim, followed by decision losses to Talita Alencar and Jamey-Lyn Horth. Before that skid, she had won four of five, including decisions over Emily Ducote, Kanako Murata, Maria Oliveira and Jinh Yu Frey.
