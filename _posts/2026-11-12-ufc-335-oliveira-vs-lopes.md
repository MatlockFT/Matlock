UFC 335: Oliveira vs. Lopes takes place Saturday, December 12, 2026, at T-Mobile Arena in Las Vegas. Charles Oliveira defends the BMF title against Diego Lopes in the lightweight main event, while Alex Pereira faces Sergei Pavlovich in a five-round heavyweight co-main event. The main card begins at 9 p.m. ET on Paramount+.

The following sections cover the matchups currently being developed for this article. The fight card is subject to change. [Official UFC 335 event page](https://www.ufc.com/event/ufc-335).

## Charles Oliveira vs. Diego Lopes

**Lightweight — BMF title fight**

**Fighter profiles:** [Charles Oliveira (UFCStats)](http://ufcstats.com/fighter-details/07225ba28ae309b6) · [Diego Lopes (UFCStats)](http://ufcstats.com/fighter-details/f166e93d04a8c274)
