UFC 335: Oliveira vs. Lopes takes place Saturday, December 12, 2026, at T-Mobile Arena in Las Vegas. The early prelims begin at 5 p.m. ET, the prelims at 7 p.m. ET, and the main card at 9 p.m. ET on Paramount+.

Charles Oliveira defends the BMF title against Diego Lopes in the lightweight main event. Alex Pereira meets Sergei Pavlovich in a five-round heavyweight co-main event. Brandon Moreno faces Charles Johnson at flyweight, and Quentin Pasley makes his UFC debut against Junior Tafa at light heavyweight.

**Card update (November 12):** Bo Nickal said his planned fight with Joe Pyfer was moved to a February 2027 Fight Night, although the UFC's event webpage has not yet reflected the change. Maycee Barber has also withdrawn from her bout with Carli Judice. Both original matchups are preserved below as archived sections, not presented as active UFC 335 bookings. **Charles Oliveira** enters as the BMF champion after defeating Max Holloway by unanimous decision at UFC 326. His professional record includes 22 submission victories and 10 knockouts. Oliveira's offensive grappling is his most dangerous skill, but he is also willing to pressure opponents on the feet and trade at close range. He has won consecutive fights against Holloway and Mateusz Gamrot after a knockout loss to Ilia Topuria.

**Diego Lopes** is moving up from featherweight for this matchup. He has 12 knockout wins and 12 submission wins, and his record reflects an aggressive approach that can produce early finishes in either phase. He knocked out Steve Garcia in June 2026, after losing a five-round decision to Alexander Volkanovski earlier in the year. Lopes has repeatedly shown a willingness to exchange strikes and attack submissions from scrambles.
