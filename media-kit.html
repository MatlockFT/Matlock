About the project

MMA content made by a longtime fan.

MMA Matlock is an independent digital MMA project covering memorable footage, fighters, fights, techniques, and unusual moments from the history of the sport.

The project publishes archive videos assembled from existing footage and media, along with original fight-breakdown videos examining selected matchups, techniques, and significant moments.

MMA Matlock is not affiliated with a promotion, broadcaster, fighter, athletic commission, or media company.

Background

Independent MMA experience

MMA Matlock is operated by a longtime MMA fan who previously worked as a camera operator for regional MMA events in the Upper Midwest.

The project is maintained independently and is produced primarily for enjoyment, documentation, and discussion of MMA history.

Archive-video reach and original-video sponsorships are presented separately. Sponsorship integrations are best suited to original breakdowns or purpose-built short-form content where the presentation can be controlled directly.

i

Reporting note

Figures come from native platform analytics reports supplied in July 2026. Platform definitions and reporting periods differ, and the same person may view content on more than one platform. For that reason, individual platform figures should not be added together and described as a unique combined audience.

Public figures are rounded for presentation. Additional analytics can be provided privately when relevant to a specific partnership.
