About the project

MMA media with a distinct point of view.

MMA Matlock covers memorable footage, fighters, fights, techniques, and unusual moments from across the history of the sport.

The editorial mix combines short-form edits of sourced classic footage with original fight breakdowns examining matchups, tactics, techniques, and significant moments.

MMA Matlock is not affiliated with a promotion, broadcaster, fighter, athletic commission, or media company.

Background

Production experience, independent voice

MMA Matlock is operated by a longtime MMA fan who previously worked as a camera operator for regional MMA events in the Upper Midwest.

The project combines that production background with long-term knowledge of the sport and an independent editorial perspective.

Classic-clip reach and original-video sponsorships are presented separately. Sponsorship integrations are best suited to original breakdowns or purpose-built short-form content where the presentation can be controlled directly.
