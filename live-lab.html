How it works

01

We track the channels

Selected MMA promotions and legitimate broadcast partners are checked for public YouTube fight streams.

02

Live fights surface here

When an eligible broadcast goes live, it appears in the player and the Live Now section automatically.

03

The next card takes over

If another tracked event is still live when the current stream ends, the player can move to the next available card.

Availability varies by event, territory and promotion. Some channels only offer prelims or selected fights. This page only surfaces legitimate public YouTube broadcasts and does not bypass paywalls, memberships or geographic restrictions.
