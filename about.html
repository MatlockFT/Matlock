About MMA Matlock

Fight breakdowns from a fan who does the homework.

MMA Matlock is an independent MMA site and channel focused on original fight breakdowns, predictions, commentary, and selected archival fight content. I watch tape, compare statistics and reporting, and write about the details I think matter in a matchup.

What I make

Original analysis and MMA media

Fight breakdowns

Original written and video analysis built around fight footage, matchup tendencies, statistics, recent form, and the specific questions that could decide a fight.

Classic MMA clip edits

Interesting moments selected from sourced older MMA footage, interviews, and broadcasts, then reformatted for short-form video with captions and visual adjustments.

Background

Independent, not affiliated with a promotion

I am a longtime MMA fan sharing my own observations, research, and opinions. MMA Matlock is independently run and is not affiliated with the UFC or another fight promotion.

I previously worked as a camera operator for regional MMA events in the Upper Midwest, which gave me some behind-the-scenes experience around live fight production.

When a breakdown uses outside statistics, reporting, quotes, or other research, I link the source where practical. Predictions and interpretations are my own opinion, and articles may be updated when new information becomes available.

Editorial approach

What readers can expect

The goal is not to pretend every prediction is certain. I try to separate what the footage and numbers show from what I am speculating about, then explain why I reached a particular pick.

If a factual error is found, I want it corrected. You can reach MMA Matlock through the contact page.
