About MMA Matlock

MMA content made by a fan.

MMA Matlock is a fan-run project featuring original fight breakdowns, edited clips from classic MMA footage, and other combat-sports content. This is something I make because I enjoy the sport.

What I make

Videos and breakdowns

Classic MMA clip edits

Interesting moments selected from sourced older MMA footage, interviews, and broadcasts, then reformatted for short-form video with captions and visual adjustments.

Fight breakdown videos

My own videos looking at specific fights, matchups, techniques, and moments that I find interesting.

Background

A fan with some time around the sport

I am not presenting myself as a professional analyst or an MMA expert. I am a longtime fan sharing my own observations and opinions.

I also previously worked as a camera operator for regional MMA events in the Upper Midwest, which gave me some behind-the-scenes experience around live fight production.

MMA Matlock is independent and mostly exists because making this kind of content is fun.
