Matchmaking rules & board overrides

Recommendations use current availability and rankings. Missing historical ranks stay unknown. UFC profile history can be incomplete; every matchup shows its evidence.

How suggestions are scored

Hierarchy fit 30 · trajectory 20 · availability 15 · fresh matchup 15 · progression 10 · timing 5 · story 5. Rematches require a documented case or your override. Booked, inactive, unavailable and incompatible-division fighters are excluded. Auto matchmaking uses a deterministic best-first search with a bounded search budget; it does not guarantee the global optimum.
