# Share-card V6 assets

The GoBold display outlines in `assets/share-gobold.mjs` are generated from the existing `Gobold Bold.woff` using `node scripts/build-share-gobold.mjs`. The same outline module renders exact letterforms in Sharp and in the browser fallback. Use `--check` to verify freshness.

`assets/textures/otd-gaffer-tape-v1.png` was generated with the built-in image-generation tool, then its transparent margins were trimmed and it was resized to 1600 pixels wide. It is an original material texture; all event imagery and all typography are composited separately.

Generation prompt: A single long horizontal strip of real matte black gaffer tape, scanned straight down, on transparent alpha. Straight, nearly parallel long edges, hand-ripped fibrous short ends, black threads, shallow compression creases, fine cloth weave and subdued charcoal highlights. Nearly black, no shiny metallic appearance. No lettering, logos, white halo, paper, objects or shadows. Flat middle area for white type. Photographic material scan, not a vector polygon or wavy shape.

Composition: a narrow masthead, image field extended to the bottom of the card, close fighter portraits, slim photo edges, individually fitted GoBold title strips, and one small credit line on black. Post, story and square share the same hierarchy while fitting their image dimensions independently.
