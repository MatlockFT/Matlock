01

Information that may be processed

You can read the public content on this website without creating an account or submitting a profile.

The website’s hosting, security, advertising, and delivery services may automatically process technical information when a page is requested. This may include information such as:

  • IP address
  • Browser and device type
  • Requested page and referring page
  • Date and time of the request
  • Approximate geographic region
  • Error, security, and performance information

This type of information may be used to deliver the site, maintain security, identify technical problems, prevent abuse, and understand general website performance.

02

Advertising, cookies, and similar technologies

This website may display advertising through Google AdSense or other advertising partners.

Third-party vendors may place or read cookies on your browser or use related technologies, including web beacons and IP-address information, in connection with advertising delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, frequency control, reporting, and personalization where permitted.

Advertising may be personalized or non-personalized depending on your location, consent choices, browser settings, and the advertising services available at the time of your visit.

03

Consent and advertising choices

Where required, visitors may be shown a consent or privacy message before certain advertising technologies are used. This message may allow visitors to accept, reject, or manage available choices.

You can also manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking or deleting cookies may affect some advertising, preference, or measurement features.

External websites and services

The site links to services operated by third parties, including social networks, video and podcast platforms, merchandise providers, advertisers, and other websites.

When you follow one of those links, the third party may process information according to its own privacy policy, cookie practices, account settings, and terms. MMA Matlock does not control those external services.

05

Information sent by email

When you email MMA Matlock, the message may include your email address, name, message contents, attachments, and any other information you choose to provide.

That information may be used to read and respond to the inquiry, evaluate a proposal, address a correction, retain relevant business records, or protect against abuse.

Do not send sensitive personal, financial, medical, or identity information that is not necessary for the inquiry.

06

This website is intended for a general audience and is not designed as a service directed specifically toward children.

A parent or guardian who believes a child has submitted personal information directly to MMA Matlock may request that the information be reviewed or deleted.
