Privacy Policy

Last updated November 17, 2026

Information collected

You can read public articles on mmamatlock.com without an account.

Hosting, security, advertising, and website services may process basic technical information when you visit, including:

  • IP address and approximate location
  • Browser and device information
  • Pages visited and referring pages
  • Time and date of requests
  • Error, performance, and security logs

This information may be used to operate the site, maintain security, prevent abuse, and resolve technical problems.

Advertising and cookies

Matlock may display ads through Google AdSense or other advertising partners. These providers may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, and similar technologies to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and personalize advertising where permitted.

Ads may be personalized or non-personalized depending on your location, consent choices, and browser settings.

Your choices

Where required, a consent message may let you accept, reject, or manage advertising cookies. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, although some features may be affected.

Paid memberships

The optional service at members.mmamatlock.com requires an email address. Resend sends one-time sign-in codes. The service stores your email address, account and session identifiers, membership status, renewal dates, and Stripe subscription identifiers. Session cookies expire after approximately 30 days; signing out ends the active session.

Stripe handles checkout, payment methods, invoices, renewals, and cancellations. Matlock does not receive or store your full card number. Stripe processes billing information under its privacy policy. Cloudflare provides hosting and private article storage.

Membership records may be kept as needed to provide the service, handle billing or security issues, and meet legal or accounting requirements. For questions about account information or deletion, email fighttalkmma@protonmail.com. Deleting account data does not automatically cancel an active subscription. Cancel future renewals through the membership billing portal.

See the membership terms for pricing and billing details.

External websites

Links to social media, video platforms, advertisers, shops, and other websites take you to services that have their own privacy policies. Matlock does not control how those services handle information.

Email

If you contact Matlock by email, your message may include your name, email address, attachments, and anything else you send. That information may be used to respond, handle corrections or proposals, keep relevant records, or prevent abuse.

Please do not send sensitive personal, financial, medical, or identity information unless it is necessary.

Children's privacy

This is a general-audience website, not a service directed at children. Parents or guardians may request review or deletion of personal information a child has sent directly to Matlock.

Changes to this policy

This policy may change when the website's services or practices change. The date above shows when it was last updated.
